I trace the lines upon my face,
And look for who I used to be,
A stranger in a quiet place,
A shadow where a soul should go.
I gave away the vital parts,
To please the crowd, to keep the peace,
And drifted far from quiet hearts,
To watch my own reflection cease.
Yet underneath the heavy stone,
A tiny ember starts to glow,
No longer lost and all alone,
I take the time to heal and grow.
