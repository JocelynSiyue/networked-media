She feared the outside world
The pandemic had killed thousands
Hundreds of thousands
She stayed in for over two years


People coaxed and cajoled her
She was lonely but would not venture out
Safe in her nest, she reread her books
She wanted to be safe


Two more years went by
She is depressed but cannot reach out
People scare her now
She is secluded, living a half life


Five more years of self-imposed hell
It was a relief the day the plane hit her building
killing her instantly
